Cν • The Contronym

A contronym is one lexical form capable of resolving into meanings that oppose one another. The word stays fixed. The surrounding context changes which semantic charge becomes active.

meaning⁻ ← Cν → meaning⁺

The transverse axis represents semantic expansion along related meanings. The conjugate axis represents the transformation connecting opposed readings. Their intersection produces the gold vertex: the lexical state shared by both.

Why the Majorana?

The Majorana analogy treats the contronym as a word that contains a relation to its own opposite. It is not literally particle physics. It is a conceptual model for visualizing semantic self-conjugacy: one word remains invariant while context reverses the interpretation.

How to Play

Begin with one clue on each semantic branch. Reveal additional hints only when needed. Use both sides of the graph to determine which single word can satisfy the apparently contradictory readings. Then collapse the clue by entering that word at the vertex.
