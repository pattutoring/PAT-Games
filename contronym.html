The Contronym Model

A contronym is a single lexical state capable of resolving into opposing meanings. The two semantic charges occupy opposite branches, while the word itself sits at the neutral vertex.

− Conjugate Opposing or inverse semantic charge.
✦ Vertex The neutral lexical object capable of expressing both.
+ Transverse Similar or affirmative semantic charge.
