WORDSMITH

Read the words. Map the relations.

Poetry is more than a collection of literary devices. Words interact through sound, structure, context, expectation and meaning. Wordsmith lets you annotate those interactions, compare them with an author's intended map, visualize how the relations fit together, and explore the structure of your own writing.

THE WORDSMITH PRINCIPLE

Don't Just Name the Device — Follow What It Does

Finding a metaphor, rhyme or pun is only the beginning. A stronger analysis asks: what transformation does the device create, what meanings does it connect, and what happens to the larger structure because that relation exists?
