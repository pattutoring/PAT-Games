EVOLUTION LINE

Phosphate Evolution

Successive deprotonation removes hydrogen as the environment becomes more basic.

H₃PO₄ → H₂PO₄⁻ → HPO₄²⁻ → PO₄³⁻
🐙
EVOLUTION LINE

Water Evolution

Gain or lose protons and the hydrogen-oxygen species changes identity.

H₃O⁺ ⇌ H₂O ⇌ OH⁻
🐦‍⬛ → 🌾
EVOLUTION LINE

Crow → Scarecrow

Chromate and dichromate move through an equilibrium as their chemical environment changes.

CrO₄²⁻ ⇌ Cr₂O₇²⁻
🧬 Evolution is chemistry in motion.

These are not unrelated formulas. Each family shows how one chemical structure transforms into another as its environment changes.
