Phosphate Porcupine Evolution

The same Porcupine family changes charge as hydrogen is progressively removed in increasingly basic environments.

Water Evolution

Gain or lose protons and the hydrogen-oxygen species changes identity.

H₃O⁺ ⇌ H₂O ⇌ OH⁻

Crow → Scarecrow
