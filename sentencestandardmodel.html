SENTENCE STANDARD MODEL

Meaning interacts. Syntax expresses.

A sentence is not treated as the primitive object. It is the visible syntactic expression of a deeper relational interaction: lexical states enter a field, exchange semantic and syntactic carriers, transform through conjugation, and collapse into sentence form.

THE MODEL

From Meaning Interaction to Sentence Form

The Sentence Standard Model separates underlying relational meaning from its charged syntactic realization. Both semantic and syntactic structure exist at every scale, but the model lets us move from transformations of meaning down into the actual linguistic objects that express them.

Context / Higgs Field Context determines which semantic possibilities acquire effective relevance or “mass.”
Semantic Mass A lexical state can carry different amounts of relational content depending on the field surrounding it.
Syntactic Charge Grammar constrains how an expression may interact, attach, agree or transform.
Conservation A sentence may change form while preserving core semantic relations.
Reversibility A visible sentence can be decomposed back into the relations that generated it.
Scale Transformations of meaning can be resolved into lower-level linguistic objects, operators and interaction vertices.
INTERACTION PARTICLES

Carriers of Meaning and Form

Different mediators perform different linguistic jobs. The important distinction is not merely which word appears, but what kind of relation was exchanged to produce the new state.

Z⁰

Semantic Z Boson

A massless neutral semantic carrier. It transmits relational meaning without imposing syntactic charge and can seed a Lexical Quantum Gravity loop.

W±

W Bosons

Charged syntactic mediators. W interactions convert an underlying lexical state into a grammatically charged realization.

γ

Syntactic Photon

Pure uncharged syntactic expression. It propagates form without changing the underlying semantic charge.

C

CQD

Conjugation Chromodynamics. It tracks conjugate grammatical states such as tense, agreement, person, number and related expression changes.

⟳

LQG Loop

Lexical Quantum Gravity closes relations into a stable lexical geometry. Green loops represent form generated from repeated relational constraints.
