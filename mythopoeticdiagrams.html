MYTHOPOETIC DIAGRAMS

When the function becomes the form.

Stories do not merely contain events. They contain relationships: oppositions, repetitions, transformations, convergences, absences, exaggerations and continuous character development. Mythopoetic Diagrams maps those structures as mathematical forms.

NARRATIVE GEOMETRY

Stories Have Shape

A narrative can be treated as a relational system. Characters can act as FOILs, moments can behave as zeroes, reversals can create an Ironic Axis of Symmetry, and continuous development can be tracked as a function through narrative space.

🔴 EVEN • MESON
🔵 ODD • BARYON

Even forms emphasize paired or dichotomous relations. Odd forms emphasize continuous development through multiple narrative identities or states.
