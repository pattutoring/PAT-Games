THE TWAIN EDITOR PRINCIPLE

A Warning Is Not a Verdict

Style rules are diagnostic tools, not commandments. An adjective may be exactly right. Repetition may be deliberate. A cliché may be transformed into a joke. The editor's job is to make you notice the choice.
